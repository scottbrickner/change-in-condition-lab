Change in Condition Charting Lab

Each case opens in a mock of the chart you use every shift. Catch what changed, see the patient first, escalate through your unit's chain, and chart what you found and who you told, at the times it actually happened. A coach gives feedback as you go, and the debrief reviews every action you took.

How it works

  • Four stages: chart review, deterioration, intervention and reassessment, handoff.
  • Each new chart screen during review, and each bedside action (counting, rechecking, adjusting), costs sim time.
  • Completing the case is the pass. The 100-point score is feedback, not a grade.

Escalation routes

Floor / Tele: primary team or cross-cover → RRT (dial 77) whenever CP5-105 criteria are met or there's no response in 10 minutes.

ICU: ICU resident/APP → fellow → attending, plus the specialty that owns the device or drip. RRT isn't the ICU route.
